STACKLY SPORTS TURF & FIELD RENTALS

Searching for the ideal multi-use space for your next event? Stackly Sports & Events Center provides top-notch indoor and outdoor Turf & Field options tailored to meet a wide range of needs. Whether you're hosting team practices, renting space for leagues, or planning film and photo shoots, Stackly has the perfect venue for you.

RENTAL RESTRICTIONS

At this time, court and field rentals are only available to organizations that provide their own liability insurance. A coach must be on-site for the entire duration of the rental. Please note that rentals are not available for individuals or field trip groups.

STACKLY FIELD HOUSE

Our expansive Field House offers 20,000 square feet of versatile event space, perfect for both sports and non-sports activities. The space includes hardwood courts and indoor turf, separated by a curtain that can be lifted to create one large, open area. With a capacity of up to 1,500 people, the Field House is ideal for large events, sports tournaments, and more.

OUTDOOR FIELDS

SWIM FOR THE FUTURE

The Swim for the Future scholarship was established in memory of Andrew Fisher and Doug Irgang, two beloved members of the Asphalt Green Unified Aquatics (AGUA) Masters swim team who tragically perished on September 11, 2001. Since its inception, the scholarship has awarded over 400 scholarships, totaling nearly $1 million, to help talented athletes on our competitive swim team, AGUA. Your generous donation supports scholarships for AGUA swimmers, as well as participants in our feeder programs, including the Swim League. Every contribution is tax-deductible and directly helps shape the future of our swimmers.
