About

WE HELP PEOPLE FIND A PLACE TO BELONG

Stackly Sports Club is a nonprofit organization that provides high-quality sports, swim, and fitness instruction and programs for children and adults. With world-class facilities and expert coaches, we offer diverse services catering to individuals of all skill levels — from first-time exercisers and new swimmers to elite athletes training for their next championship.

Stackly functions on various levels. At our facilities, we offer memberships to use our top-notch fitness center and pools. We also run instructional classes and programs for children and adults on a term basis four times throughout the year — winter, spring, summer, and fall. Programs include infant swim and diving classes, youth basketball leagues, and private Pilates Reformer classes.

Our Mission

WE TRANSFORM LIVES AND BUILD COMMUNITY THROUGH SPORTS, FITNESS, AND PLAY FOR ALL

Lasting personal and social growth comes when we all work together. That's why strengthening the community is our cause. Every day, we work side-by-side with our neighbors to make sure that everyone, regardless of age, income, or background, has the opportunity to learn, grow and thrive.

Youth Development

Each year we help thousands of youth cultivate the values, skills, and relationships that lead to positive behaviors, better health, and educational achievement. Our programs and services help youth achieve their full potential.

Healthy Living

We promote healthy living in spirit, mind, and body. We offer state-of-the-art fitness equipment, unlimited group exercise classes, and a variety of programs and activities that help people create healthy habits, become more active, and prevent chronic disease.

Social Responsibility

We are deeply committed to fostering social responsibility in the communities where we live, work, and play. We take great pride in providing support to community members from all walks of life, helping them reach their full potential and live a balanced, purposeful life.

Our Story

TRANSFORMING LIVES FOR OVER 50 YEARS

Since our early beginnings, Stackly Sports Club has been a force for good in the community, leading and facilitating positive impact through sport. Over the years, we have grown and evolved to meet the changing needs of our members. As a strong and vibrant club, we are committed to improving the quality of life for the more than 260,000 individuals we serve every year.
